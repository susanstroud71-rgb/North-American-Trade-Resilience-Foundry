### 2. SCOPE OF THE REPOSITORY

The North American Trade Resilience Foundry contains or may contain independent materials concerning subjects including:

* North American trade policy and economic relations;
* Canada–United States–Mexico trade relations;
* CUSMA/USMCA modernization and reform;
* the proposed North American Economic Security and Trade Modernization Agreement (NAESTMA);
* tariff and non-tariff trade measures;
* trade disputes and trade-war scenarios;
* economic coercion and economic-security risks;
* diplomatic de-escalation and dispute prevention;
* negotiation strategies and bargaining frameworks;
* proposed treaty provisions, chapters, annexes, schedules, side letters, protocols, declarations, and related instruments;
* sector-specific trade arrangements;
* supply-chain security and economic resilience;
* industrial, agricultural, energy, critical-minerals, transportation, technology, procurement, labor, environmental, and other trade-related policy;
* contingency planning and responses to potential trade disruptions;
* proposed compromises, concessions, safeguards, off-ramps, stabilization mechanisms, and confidence-building measures;
* analyses of existing or potential governmental policies, negotiating positions, demands, responses, and areas of disagreement; and
* other diplomatic, economic, legal-policy, institutional, humanitarian, or international-cooperation concepts associated with North American trade and economic security.

The inclusion of these subjects is descriptive of the repository's subject matter and does not represent or imply governmental authorization, adoption, sponsorship, endorsement, or official status.
